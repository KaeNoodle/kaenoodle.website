COLOURWAY 118 · SHOCK PINK

Fursuit making

Heads, paws and full builds. Below is how a head comes together, start to finish.

Replace with a build photo
fursuits/head-01.jpg
fursuits/head-02.jpg
fursuits/head-03.jpg

  1. Pattern & base

    Balaclava base, then foam carved down to the shapes I want. This is where the character's expression gets decided.

  2. Eyes and teeth

    Buckram eyes and resin or foam teeth. Small parts, most of the personality.

  3. Furring

    Pattern taped over the foam, transferred to fur, sewn and glued. Slowest step by a distance.

  4. Airbrush & finishing

    Shading, trimming the pile down around the muzzle, fans and vents fitted.
